## 1 Study Overview

NVK-778 is an oral selective TYK2 inhibitor under investigation for
moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis. This is a randomized, double-blind,
placebo-controlled Phase II study enrolling approximately 180 adult patients
across two cohorts. Cohort A receives NVK-778 15 mg once daily; Cohort B
receives NVK-778 30 mg once daily. Treatment continues for 24 weeks with a
12-week safety follow-up period.

This protocol governs Trial 778 only. It shares no eligibility criteria, dosing
rules, or visit schedule with any other study.

## 4.1 Eligibility - Live Vaccines

Administration of **live or live-attenuated vaccines** is **prohibited** from 28
days prior to the first dose of NVK-778 through 90 days after the final dose.
Because TYK2 inhibition attenuates interferon-mediated antiviral responses, the
immunogenicity and safety of live vaccines cannot be assured during treatment.
Inactivated and mRNA vaccines are permitted at any point and do not require an
interruption of study drug. Investigators must confirm and document vaccination
history at screening. A patient who has received a live vaccine within the 28-day
window may be rescreened once the full interval has elapsed.

## 5.2 Eligibility - Concomitant Metformin

Concomitant **metformin** is **permitted without a dose ceiling** for patients
enrolling under this protocol. NVK-778 is not metabolized by CYP2C8 and is not an
OCT2 or MATE substrate, so no pharmacokinetic interaction with metformin is
anticipated. Patients on metformin should continue their established regimen; no
stabilization period is required prior to the first dose. The medication must be
recorded in the concomitant medication log. Investigators need not adjust the
metformin dose on account of study participation.

## 6 Washout Period, Cohort B

Patients enrolling into **Cohort B** who have received prior systemic biologic
therapy for psoriasis must observe a **washout of 5 half-lives or 12 weeks,
whichever is longer**, before the first dose of NVK-778. Topical corticosteroids
must be discontinued at least 14 days prior to baseline. Phototherapy must be
discontinued at least 28 days prior to baseline. Cohort A patients, who are
biologic-naive by definition, require no biologic washout but are subject to the
same topical and phototherapy intervals.

## 7 Follow-up Visit Timing

Follow-up visits occur at Weeks 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, and 24 during treatment, then
at Weeks 30 and 36 during safety follow-up. Each visit has a window of **+/- 3
days** relative to the scheduled day, calculated from the date of first dose. A
visit falling outside its window is recorded as a protocol deviation and must be
reported to the sponsor within 5 business days.
