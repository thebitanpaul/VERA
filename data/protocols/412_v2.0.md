# Trial 412 — Protocol Version 2.0

**Study title:** A Phase 2, Open-Label Study of Investigational Agent VRA-412 in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors
**Sponsor protocol number:** VRA-412
**Phase:** 2
**Effective date:** 01 February 2024
**Status:** Superseded
**Supersedes:** Version 1.0

## 1. Synopsis

Trial 412 is a Phase 2, open-label, multicenter study evaluating the safety and
preliminary efficacy of the investigational agent VRA-412 in adults with advanced
or metastatic solid tumors who have progressed on, or are intolerant to, standard
therapy. Patients are enrolled into disease-specific cohorts (Cohort A and Cohort B)
and receive VRA-412 in continuous 28-day cycles until disease progression,
unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent. The primary endpoint is objective
response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1; secondary endpoints include progression-free
survival (PFS), duration of response (DOR), and safety.

## 5.2 Eligibility — Concomitant Metformin

Because of the potential for overlapping metabolic effects and the theoretical risk
of altered glycemic control during study treatment, patients receiving **metformin**
(as monotherapy or in any fixed-dose combination) at the time of screening are
**excluded** from enrollment under this version of the protocol.

Investigators must review each candidate's concomitant medication list at screening.
A patient taking metformin may be reconsidered for eligibility only if the medication
is clinically discontinued and an appropriate washout has elapsed prior to the first
dose, at the discretion of the treating physician and in consultation with the medical
monitor. No exception to the metformin exclusion is permitted without documented
sponsor approval.

## 6. Washout Period, Cohort B

Patients enrolling into **Cohort B** must observe a **14-day washout** period from
their most recent prior systemic anticancer therapy before receiving the first dose
of VRA-412. The washout is measured from the last administration of prior therapy to
Cycle 1 Day 1. Patients who cannot complete the full 14-day washout — for example due
to rapid clinical progression — must be discussed with the medical monitor before
dosing. Palliative radiotherapy to a non-target lesion is not subject to the washout
requirement.

## 7. Follow-up Visit Timing

A post-treatment follow-up (safety) visit is required for all patients following
discontinuation of study treatment. Effective with this version of the protocol, the
follow-up visit window has been revised from the previous 28 (±3) days to
**30 (±5) days after the last dose** of VRA-412. This change widens the visit window to
improve scheduling feasibility across sites while preserving timely safety capture.
At this visit, adverse events, concomitant medications, and clinical laboratory
parameters are assessed, and any ongoing treatment-related toxicity is followed until
resolution or stabilization. Patients who discontinue for reasons other than
progression additionally enter long-term survival follow-up per Section 9.
