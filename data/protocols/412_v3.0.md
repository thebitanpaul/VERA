# Trial 412 — Protocol Version 3.0

**Study title:** A Phase 2, Open-Label Study of Investigational Agent VRA-412 in Patients with Advanced Solid Tumors
**Sponsor protocol number:** VRA-412
**Phase:** 2
**Effective date:** 01 March 2025
**Status:** Active
**Supersedes:** Version 2.0

## 1. Synopsis

Trial 412 is a Phase 2, open-label, multicenter study evaluating the safety and
preliminary efficacy of the investigational agent VRA-412 in adults with advanced
or metastatic solid tumors who have progressed on, or are intolerant to, standard
therapy. Patients are enrolled into disease-specific cohorts (Cohort A and Cohort B)
and receive VRA-412 in continuous 28-day cycles until disease progression,
unacceptable toxicity, or withdrawal of consent. The primary endpoint is objective
response rate (ORR) per RECIST v1.1; secondary endpoints include progression-free
survival (PFS), duration of response (DOR), and safety.

## 5.2 Eligibility — Concomitant Metformin

Concomitant **metformin** is **permitted** for patients enrolling under this version of
the protocol, provided the **total daily dose does not exceed 2000 mg/day**. Patients
must be on a stable metformin dose for at least 14 days prior to the first dose of
VRA-412, and the dose and schedule must be recorded in the concomitant medication log
and monitored throughout treatment. Investigators should remain alert for signs of
altered glycemic control and manage per local standard of care.

This provision was **amended from Version 1.0, under which metformin use was an exclusion
criterion**; accumulated safety experience supported allowing metformin at doses up to
2000 mg/day. Patients requiring more than 2000 mg/day of metformin remain ineligible.

## 6. Washout Period, Cohort B

Patients enrolling into **Cohort B** must observe a **14-day washout** period from
their most recent prior systemic anticancer therapy before receiving the first dose
of VRA-412. The washout is measured from the last administration of prior therapy to
Cycle 1 Day 1. Patients who cannot complete the full 14-day washout — for example due
to rapid clinical progression — must be discussed with the medical monitor before
dosing. Palliative radiotherapy to a non-target lesion is not subject to the washout
requirement.

## 7. Follow-up Visit Timing

A post-treatment follow-up (safety) visit is required for all patients following
discontinuation of study treatment. The follow-up visit must occur at
**30 (±5) days after the last dose** of VRA-412 (unchanged from Version 2.0). At this
visit, adverse events, concomitant medications, and clinical laboratory parameters are
assessed, and any ongoing treatment-related toxicity is followed until resolution or
stabilization. Patients who discontinue for reasons other than progression additionally
enter long-term survival follow-up per Section 9.
